# VideoPlayer Studio & Live Dubbing Platform

## Product Truth
VideoPlayer is a professional web media player and real-time AI dubbing platform. It combines high-fidelity local video streaming, playlist management, and instant live multilingual dubbing powered by Faster-Whisper, Demucs music separation, and Zero-Shot voice cloning.

## Primary Users & Jobs
- **Content Creators, Video Editors & Media Consumers**: Playing videos locally, testing dubbing tracks, switching languages seamlessly, managing video libraries and playlists.
- **Job to Be Done**: Deliver instantaneous, latency-free playback with broadcast-quality visuals, clean audio track selection, and intuitive controls.

## Platform
- `web` (Angular 18, Tailwind CSS, HTML5 Video & Web Audio, Spring Boot 3.2 backend).

## Design Direction & Intent
- **Aesthetic**: *Épuré & Professionnel* (Minimalist, studio-grade, refined dark workspace).
- **Mode**: `Operate` (App UI, tool, media dashboard). The interface disappears to let the media content take center stage.
- **Key Characteristics**:
  - Balanced zinc-slate dark theme with subtle border elevation instead of harsh drop shadows.
  - Modern typography with clean letter-spacing and tabular numerical figures for timecodes.
  - Crimson/ruby refined accent (`#e11d48` / `#f43f5e`) for active states, avoiding eye-straining oversaturated reds.
  - Restrained micro-interactions and smooth transitions (150–200ms).
